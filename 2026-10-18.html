JevAgentHarness routes routine choices to a smaller decision model only above a confidence threshold fitted to the agent's own trajectories; otherwise the main LLM decides. Why it matters: The useful idea is calibrated delegation with an explicit fallback—not simply replacing a large model with a smaller one.
