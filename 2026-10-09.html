The paper trains a dedicated user simulator on human conversations and realistic behaviour patterns, then reports stronger agent generalisation across unseen simulated users; coaching turns the simulator’s private reasoning into additional training feedback. @omarsar0 · 2026-10-09 🔁 Repost

The Agent Plasticity paper measures how efficiently experience becomes gains on held-out tasks, not simply the final score. Agents carry reusable artefacts into fresh runs; high endpoint capability and efficient learning can diverge, and gains often transfer only partly outside the training regime. Failure analysis separates not reusing a relevant artefact from reusing one whose quality, generalisation or application is inadequate.

Why it matters: Saving memories or skills is not evidence of learning: test their reuse, held-out benefit and acquisition cost separately.
