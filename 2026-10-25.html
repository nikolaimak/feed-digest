Self-improvement looked much weaker on held-out games than on development games. The study described by Elvis tests complete harness rewrites across 27 lineages: two revisions add only 2.80 points on held-out scores, with a 95% interval crossing zero; some cost reductions also reduce performance. Human-steered harnesses do better, so the result is about the reliability of this optimisation method, not proof that better designs are impossible.
