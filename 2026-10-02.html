Elvis contrasts the coordination bottleneck in AgentWorld—where fewer than a third of team actions help finish the task—with Google Research’s Cogentic approach to proof discovery. The reported expert-checked research results suggest a more concrete coordination pattern than simply adding agents, not a guarantee that it transfers to every task.
