@omarsar0 · 🔁 Repost

Recurrent Looped Transformer (RLT) proposes carrying state through every prompt and response token. A causal encoder supplies global KV memory; the recurrent decoder combines each token with the previous final hidden state and a sliding activation cache. The same transition is intended for pretraining, supervised tuning, sampling and RL replay, which rebuilds state under current weights. This is an unmeasured design proposal: reasoning gains, hardware speedups and RL scaling have not been demonstrated.

RLT design repository
Why it matters: The interesting idea is persistent recurrent computation with fixed per-token work, not an established benchmark win.

Richard Sutton recommends the Math and AI declaration. Its authors argue that solving famous problems is a proxy for mathematics’ deeper goals: conceptual understanding, new methods, attribution and training future mathematicians. They warn that rushed AI-generated solutions and benchmark incentives can damage those goals, while recognising that AI could accelerate genuine understanding. The declaration calls on mathematicians, AI companies and society to address the mismatch.

Read the declaration
Why it matters: The distinction between producing an answer and developing understanding applies well beyond mathematics.
